MatterCoach — Structure of Matter

MatterCoach is an AI-assisted physics tutor for engineering students. It supports conceptual learning, step-by-step problem solving and feedback on students' work.

The initial collection includes Problem Sheet 1, with 12 consecutively numbered exercises on thermal radiation, the photoelectric effect, photons and matter waves.

Students can view complete problem statements, ask questions, request hints and submit calculations for feedback. AI responses may contain errors and should be checked against course materials.

Project contact: Filipe Joaquim, Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon.

Status: The ChatGPT plugin is being prepared for public review; public access is not yet available.
